Destinations

Where UAE travellers go, and what to know before you book.

Popular trips from the UAE

Flight times are approximate non-stop times from Dubai. Visa requirements depend on your nationality and UAE residency, so ask us to check before you book.

Istanbul & Cappadocia, Turkey

Flight time
About 4.5 hours
Best time
April to June, September to November
Good for
Families, couples, food and history

The Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, the Grand Bazaar and a Bosphorus cruise in Istanbul, often combined with two or three nights in Cappadocia for the hot-air balloons.

Enquire about Turkey

Maldives

Flight time
About 4 hours
Best time
November to April
Good for
Honeymoons, resorts, diving and snorkelling

Island resorts with beach or overwater villas. The transfer from Malé to your resort, by speedboat or seaplane, is a big part of the price, so we include it in every quote.

Enquire about the Maldives

Tbilisi, Georgia

Flight time
About 3.5 hours
Best time
May to October; December to March for snow
Good for
Short breaks, mountains, food and wine

A relaxed city break with day trips to Kazbegi in the mountains and the Kakheti wine region. Gudauri is popular for skiing in winter.

Enquire about Georgia

Phuket, Krabi & Bangkok, Thailand

Flight time
About 6 hours
Best time
November to April
Good for
Beaches, island hopping, families

A few nights in Bangkok followed by the beaches of Phuket or Krabi, with day trips to Phi Phi and the islands of Phang Nga Bay.

Enquire about Thailand

Paris & Europe

Flight time
About 7 hours to Paris
Best time
May to September
Visa
Schengen visa needed for most nationalities

Multi-city trips across France, Switzerland, Italy, Austria and Germany by train. We can help with the Schengen visa application, hotel bookings and insurance for the embassy.
