About FlyDirham

FlyDirham is a travel agency based in Oud Metha, Dubai, a short walk from Oud Metha Metro Station. We book flights, hotels and holidays for individuals, families and companies across the UAE.

You can visit our office, call us or email us. We handle the itinerary, the bookings and the visa paperwork, and we're here to help if anything changes once you're travelling.
