Travel Insurance

Cover for medical costs, cancellations and lost baggage.

Cover before you go

Medical treatment abroad can be very expensive, and many visas, including Schengen visas, require travel insurance. We arrange travel insurance from established insurers for trips starting in the UAE.

What a policy can cover

  • Emergency medical treatment and hospital stays
  • Medical evacuation and repatriation
  • Trip cancellation or cutting a trip short
  • Lost, delayed or damaged baggage
  • Flight delays and missed connections
  • Personal liability

Types of plan

Single trip

Cover for one journey, from departure until you return home.

Annual multi-trip

For people who travel several times a year.

Schengen visa

Plans that meet the minimum cover required for Schengen applications.

Family

Parents and children covered under one policy.

Read the policy wording. Cover, limits and exclusions vary between plans, especially for existing medical conditions and adventure activities. We'll send you the policy document before you buy.
