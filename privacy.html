Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who we are

FlyDirham ("we", "us") is a travel agency located at Office 612, Office Court Building, Oud Metha, Dubai, United Arab Emirates operating the website flydirham.com.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Trip details needed to make bookings, such as travel dates, destinations and traveller names.
  • Passport and visa information, only when required for a booking or visa application you ask us to handle.

How we use it

We use your information to answer enquiries, make and manage bookings, process visa requests, and send you service updates about your trip. We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing

Messages sent through the forms on this website are delivered to our email by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an email forwarding service. We share booking details with airlines, hotels, insurers and visa authorities only as needed to provide the service you requested.

Retention and security

We keep data only as long as needed for the booking and our legal obligations, and protect it with reasonable technical and organisational measures.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data by emailing info@flydirham.com.
