About FlyDirham

A travel agency built for UAE residents

FlyDirham is a travel agency based in the United Arab Emirates. We help individuals, families and businesses plan and book travel, with pricing in UAE Dirhams and support from people who know the region.

We're building our own booking platform so customers can compare options, manage itineraries and get updates in one place.

  • Clear pricing in AED
  • One contact for flights, hotels and visas
  • Support for both leisure and business travel

Our mission

To make travel from the UAE simple to plan and easy to trust, with honest advice, clear prices and help whenever plans change.
