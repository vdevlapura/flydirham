Who we are

FlyDirham Travel & Tourism is a travel agency based in Oud Metha, Dubai. We book flights, hotels, holiday packages and visas for individuals, families and companies across the UAE.

Most of our customers are UAE residents travelling home, going on holiday, or bringing family over to visit. We also look after business travel for companies that want one place to handle their staff trips.

Our office is a short walk from Oud Metha Metro Station, so you can come in and plan your trip face to face, or do everything by phone and email if that's easier.

How we work

Prices in AED

Quotes are in dirhams, with taxes and fees shown up front, so there are no surprises at payment.

Honest advice

We'll tell you if a cheaper fare has strict rules, or if a hotel is far from where you need to be.

One point of contact

The same team handles your flights, hotel, visa and insurance, before and during the trip.

Help when plans change

Delays, cancellations and date changes happen. Contact us and we'll deal with the airline or hotel.

What we're building

Alongside the agency, we're building our own online booking platform. Customers will be able to request quotes, view their bookings and itineraries, and get trip updates in one place, while our team keeps handling the bookings and support behind it.
