Business travel without the back-and-forth

For companies in the UAE, we handle staff travel from one place: flights, hotels, visas and insurance. Your team sends the request, we send options within your travel policy, and once approved, we book and send the documents.

What we handle

  • Flights and hotels for meetings, conferences and site visits
  • Business visas and visit visas for incoming guests
  • Group travel for exhibitions, training and team events
  • Changes at short notice when plans move
  • Travel insurance for staff

How it works for your company

One contact

A named person at FlyDirham who knows your company and your travellers.

Your travel policy

We quote within your rules for cabin class, hotel budget and approvals.

Billing in AED

Consolidated invoices for your accounts team, monthly or per trip.

Traveller profiles

Passport details and preferences kept on file so bookings are faster.

Open a corporate account

Send us your company name, trade licence copy, the contact for approvals and the email for invoices. We'll set up the account and agree payment terms with you.
