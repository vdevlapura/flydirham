Flights from the UAE

We book flights from Dubai (DXB and DWC), Sharjah (SHJ) and Abu Dhabi (AUH) on full-service and low-cost airlines, including Emirates, flydubai, Etihad, Air Arabia, and most international carriers that fly into the UAE.

Tell us where you want to go and roughly when, and we'll come back with a few options. We'll show you the price, baggage allowance, flight times and change rules for each, so you can compare properly before you book.

What we book

  • One-way, return and multi-city tickets
  • Economy, premium economy, business and first class
  • Group bookings for families, weddings, teams and events
  • Student and labour fares where airlines offer them
  • Extra baggage, seat selection and meal requests
  • Date changes, name corrections and refunds on tickets booked with us

What we need to book

  • Full names exactly as they appear in each passport
  • Travel dates, or a range if your dates are flexible
  • Passport copies for international travel
  • A mobile number and email for flight updates
Before you fly: check that your passport is valid for at least six months from your travel date, and that you have the visa you need for your destination and any transit stop. We can check this for you when you book.

Changes and cancellations

Every fare has its own rules. Some tickets can be changed or refunded for a fee, and some can't be changed at all. We'll explain the rules of your fare before you pay, and if your plans change, contact us and we'll handle it with the airline.
