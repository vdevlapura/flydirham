Holidays arranged as one trip

A holiday package puts your flights, hotel, airport transfers and sightseeing into one booking with one price. You don't have to coordinate different websites, and if a flight is delayed, there's one place to call.

We can start from a standard itinerary or build the trip around you: the number of nights, the type of hotel, how much sightseeing you want and how much free time.

A package usually includes

  • Return flights from the UAE
  • Hotel stay with breakfast, or more meals if you prefer
  • Airport pick-up and drop-off
  • Selected tours or day trips
  • Visa assistance where the destination needs one
  • Travel insurance, if you want it

Who we plan for

Families

Connecting rooms, kid-friendly hotels and a slower pace.

Couples & honeymoons

Resort stays, private transfers and special touches.

Groups

Friends, community groups and school trips with group fares.

Short trips that fit around UAE public holidays.
