Hotels chosen around your trip

Location matters more than star ratings in most cities. Tell us why you're travelling, who's coming and your budget, and we'll suggest hotels in the right area, close to your meetings, the beach, the old town or the hospital you're visiting.

We book hotels on their own or together with flights. Prices are quoted in AED, and we'll tell you clearly whether breakfast, taxes and city fees are included.

What we book

  • City hotels and business hotels
  • Beach resorts and all-inclusive stays
  • Serviced apartments for families and longer stays
  • Group room blocks for weddings, events and teams
  • UAE staycations in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Ras Al Khaimah
  • Airport transfers to and from your hotel

Things we check for you

  • Room type and bed setup for your group
  • Check-in and check-out times, and early check-in if you need it
  • Cancellation deadline and whether the rate is refundable
  • Local taxes or tourism fees paid at the hotel
Refundable or not? Non-refundable rates are cheaper, but you lose the money if plans change. If your dates aren't fixed yet, ask us for a refundable rate.
